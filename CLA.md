## Rule data specifically

Compliance rules are facts about the law, and facts are not copyrightable. What
this agreement covers for a rule contribution is the particular expression —
the JSON, the wording of a title or note. Contributing a rule does not mean you
are giving legal advice, warranting that the rule is correct, or taking on any
liability if it turns out to be wrong.

That is also why every rule carries a `citation` and a `status`: a rule you have
not verified against the primary source should be submitted as
`status: "draft"`, and that is a perfectly good contribution.
